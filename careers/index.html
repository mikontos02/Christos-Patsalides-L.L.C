Why join us

An established practice, in the heart of Nicosia

The firm was founded in 1996 by Christos Patsalides and is located close to the courts and the business centre of Nicosia.

  1. 01

    Since 1996

    A reputation for effective representation through innovative and practical solutions, built since the firm was founded.

  2. 02

    Varied, meaningful work

    Our client base includes domestic and international clients as well as the public sector, across twelve areas of practice.

  3. 03

    Legal and corporate disciplines

    Lawyers work alongside the accounting, corporate and financial advisers of Christos Patsalides Corporate Management Ltd.

  4. 04

    Careers that develop here

    Four of our partners have been with the firm since 2010 or earlier, and six of our associates trained with us before joining the legal team.

Our professional environment

Honesty, integrity and transparency are the foundation of our good name

  • Clients firstOur clients are the reason for our existence, and our attitude is shaped by the aim to be of value.
  • Equality and diversityWe support a culture of integrity and good employment practices that respects human rights, equality and diversity in our office.
  • CommunityWe are committed to providing pro bono services to people in our community who would otherwise have no access to legal help.
  • ResponsibilityWe aim to minimise our impact on the environment by following high standards of environmental protection practices.

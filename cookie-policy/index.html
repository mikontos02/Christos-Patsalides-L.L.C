Privacy Policy

What are cookies?

We collect data when you visit our website via Cookies. What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files that we create when you visit our website. We store these files on your web browser and we access them every time you visit our websites. They contain information regarding your visit and activity. For instance we keep information regarding your bookings as you explore our website, without cookies every time you left the page to visit a new link your previous choices would be lost.

Session and persistent cookies

A session cookie is deleted when you close your browser. A persistent cookie remains on your device until it expires or you delete it.

Third-party cookies

While you are browsing our website some cookies may have been set by third parties which we have chosen to use as suppliers. We control their use of your data through contracts and they are only allowed to use your data strictly for the purpose we have stated.

How this website uses cookies

We checked how this website behaves in a browser on 17 October 2026, across all of its pages and with the optional map loaded.

  • This website does not set any cookies of its own.
  • It does not use analytics, performance, advertising or marketing cookies, and does not store information in your browser’s local or session storage.
  • The only optional third-party content is the map on our Contact Us page, which is loaded from Google Maps only if you choose to show it.

Third-party services

Google Maps

The map on the Contact Us page is provided by Google. Until you select “Show interactive map”, no request is made to Google Maps. Once shown, Google may set or read its own cookies and process information in line with its own policies. No cookies were set by Google Maps in our check, but this can depend on your location, browser and whether you are signed in to Google.

See how Google uses cookies and the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Fonts

Our pages load the Manrope typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser requests the font files from Google’s servers. No cookies were set by these requests in our check. Google describes its approach in the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

Links to other websites

Links to social media and other websites take you to services that operate their own cookies and privacy policies.

Managing cookies

You can manage your cookies if you so wish. They are kept on your web browser. Popular web browsers include Internet Explorer, Firefox, Chrome and Safari. Each web browser stores cookies in a slightly different file but they usually store them under Tools, Options, Privacy. You can choose your settings and how often you want cookies to be deleted. Do note that banning all cookies will make some websites difficult or impossible to navigate.

Help pages for current browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Because this website sets no optional cookies of its own, it does not have a cookie preference centre. To avoid Google Maps altogether, simply do not show the map; reloading the Contact Us page returns it to its unloaded state.

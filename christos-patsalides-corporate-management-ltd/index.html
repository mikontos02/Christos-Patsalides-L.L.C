The services below

Corporate services

What the associated company offers

Five services, several of them delivered in conjunction with the firm’s lawyers.

01

Fiduciary

In conjunction with our legal team, fiduciary services to help ensure that clients’ tax goals are achieved and their assets are protected.

  • Company incorporationRegistration of companies in Cyprus and abroad, including name approval and the filing of all required documentation.
  • Know Your ClientKYC procedures and the necessary documentation.
  • RepresentationDirectors, secretaries and nominee shareholders, and acting as bank signatories or document executors.
  • Registered officeA registered office address.
  • ResolutionsDrafting of shareholders’ and directors’ resolutions.
  • Powers of attorney
  • Re-domiciliationRe-domiciliation of companies.
02

Accounting

Accurate, compliant accounting and financial reporting, aimed at helping clients minimise operational costs and free resources to focus on business growth.

  • Bookkeeping and management accountsRecording day-to-day transactions and preparing monthly or quarterly management accounts.
  • PayrollMaking sure employees are paid on time, deductions are correctly calculated and associated taxes are filed.
  • Audit supportLiaising and coordinating with external auditors, with full support until the audit concludes.
03

Tax Services and Support

Advice and compliance services on direct and indirect taxes for local and international clients, including liaison with the tax authorities and assistance with tax investigations and procedures.

  • Tax planningStructuring transactions to add value and minimise financial risk.
  • Intellectual property regimeGuidance through the Cyprus IP regime, so clients can benefit from it and protect their IP assets.
  • Tax returnsPreparation and submission of returns, with full and timely compliance.
  • VAT and VIES registrationRegistration with the Inland Revenue for VAT and VIES submissions, and filing as appropriate.
  • DAC 6 complianceCross-border arrangement reporting for individuals, corporations and tax intermediaries.
04

Funds

Fund management and structuring, from fund formation to tax structuring and exit strategies. Together with our lawyers, we negotiate legal and commercial terms and draft all relevant documentation.

Funds is also one of the firm’s areas of practice.

05

Restructurings, Mergers and Acquisitions

Experience in every stage of M&A, from venture formation, financing and shareholders’ agreements to operation, restructuring and final exit strategies, with advice on tax, risk and jurisdictional implications.

  • Advice on structuringDue diligence, tax planning, guidance on the optimal structure of the transaction and monitoring at every stage until completion.
  • Due diligenceFinancial, tax and GDPR due diligence, in collaboration with our legal and financial experts.
  • Shareholders’ arrangements
  • Competition mattersWhere a transaction raises monopoly or competition issues: market research, compliance and risk management, and coordination with competition authorities.

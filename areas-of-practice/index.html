What we offer

Twelve areas of practice

Each area links to its detailed page, with the full scope of the work involved.

  1. 01

    Litigation↗ (current firm website)

    Our litigation team devises a case strategy that protects our clients’ interests, manages risk and reduces potential escalation, with experience in every phase from investigation and pleadings to discovery, trial and appeal.

    →
  2. 02

    Insurance↗ (current firm website)

    In our view, insurance law is a cornerstone of modern economic activity: the transfer of risk from an individual or entity to an insurer, on the terms of the insurance contract.

    →
  3. 03

    Real Estate↗ (current firm website)

    Broad expertise in commercial and private real estate transactions: due diligence, documentation and negotiation of a purchase or sale, with advice on tax and financing.

    →
  4. 04

    Mass Media and Entertainment↗ (current firm website)

    Extensive experience in the mass media market and the Cyprus Radio Television Law, from establishing and operating private radio and television stations to IP, defamation, censorship and privacy.

    →
  5. 05

    Family Law↗ (current firm website)

    Family matters require the utmost sensitivity and discretion. Where children are involved, as in parental responsibility, we safeguard our client’s rights with due deference to the interests of minor children.

    →
  6. 06

    Funds↗ (current firm website)

    Fund management and structuring, whether you are purchasing outright or investing in a business, with advice at every stage from fund formation to tax structuring and exit strategies.

    →
  7. 07

    Mergers & Acquisitions↗ (current firm website)

    Long experience in complex transactions for prospective buyers and sellers, from a memorandum of understanding to the final agreements, including legal and fiscal advice, risk assessment and due diligence.

    →
  8. 08

    Corporate and Commercial Law↗ (current firm website)

    Representation and advice on commercial and cross-border transactions under Cypriot, EU and international law, including commercial contracts, financing, e-commerce, consumer protection and intellectual property.

    →
  9. 09

    Employment & Labour Law↗ (current firm website)

    The laws, European directives and regulations and international conventions that govern employment in Cyprus, and the rights and obligations of employers and employees.

    →
  10. 10

    Immigration Law↗ (current firm website)

    Substantial experience in immigration, emigration, adjustment of status and political asylum, from visas and employment, residence and immigration permits to applications for citizenship.

    →
  11. 11

    Inheritance, Estate Planning & Wills↗ (current firm website)

    Careful, timely planning of how your property is distributed, so that assets pass in accordance with your wishes and your heirs avoid unnecessary and lengthy court proceedings.

    →
  12. 12

    Trusts↗ (current firm website)

    Setting up trusts in compliance with Cyprus regulations, including Cyprus International Trusts, and drafting trust instruments that fulfil the settlor’s wishes and keep trustees accountable.

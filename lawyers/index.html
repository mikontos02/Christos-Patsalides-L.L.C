Lawyers

Christos Patsalides

Founder and Managing Partner

Christos founded our Firm in 1996 under the name Christos Patsalides & Associates; the Firm evolved in 2008 to Christos Patsalides LLC. Christos’s strong work ethic and unwavering values have been the driving force behind the Firm’s reputation, permeating every aspect of its operations.

Christos has extensive experience in Corporate and Commercial law, Real Estate law, Media law, Tax law, and Equity & Trust law. Throughout his career, he provided valuable advice on various significant local and international transactions. Within his expertise, Christos has served on the board of multiple companies, including subsidiaries of foreign publicly listed companies and companies licenced by the Cyprus Bar Association as trustee companies and by the Cyprus Stock Exchange. Moreover, Christos served as the vice president of Cyprus’ licensing authority.

  • LLB, National Kapodistrian University of Athens, Athens, Greece, 1991
  • LLM, Shipping Law, University of Southampton, Southampton, United Kingdom 1993
  • LLM, Commercial and Company Law, UCL, London, United Kingdom 1993
  • Admitted to the Bar of Cyprus, Nicosia Chapter, in 1995

Panayiotis A. Kakkouras

Senior Partner

Panayiotis has extensive experience in all areas of Civil Law, including Contract Law, Commercial, Labor and Corporate Law as well as Administrative Law and European Law.

From 1997 to 2002, he founded and managed his own law firm “Panayiotis A. Kakkouras and Associates”. In April 2002, he was appointed by the Cabinet as Deputy Commissioner of Electronic Communications and Postal Regulations, a position he held for a six-year term until March 2008. He also served as a Board Member of the Famagusta Bar Association, the Cyprus Land Development Organization and the Prison Council. In addition, Panayiotis is the President of the “Foundation Dr. Lena Loizou Kakkouras”.

Since 2016, he has been a member of the Supreme Judicial Committee of Sports. Moreover, he is registered as a mediator for commercial and civil cases in the Ministry of Justice and Public Order registers respectively. He is also registered in the register of Arbitrators and Mediators of the Scientific and Technical Chamber of Cyprus (ETEK).

Panayiotis was a co-founder in 2008 of the law firm “Kakkouras & Panayides LLC” until its merger with the firm “Christos Patsalidis LLC” in 2024, in which he is currently a Senior Partner.

Education
  • BSc in Electrical Engineering, Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, Greece, 1990

  • Admitted to the Bar of Cyprus, Nicosia Chapter, in 1996

Avgoustinos Tsarkatzis

Supervising Partner and Deputy Head of the Litigation Department

Avgoustinos, a partner of the Firm, has been with the Firm since 2004.

He is a highly experienced and well-regarded litigation lawyer with extensive knowledge of civil and administrative law, family law and property law. Regarding family law, Avgoustinos handles divorces, child custody, alimony cases and matrimonial property disputes. In addition, Avgoustinos has a long career arguing before the Cyprus Radio Television Authority while representing television and radio stations. As a result of Avgoustinos’ involvement and experience with the mass media market in Cyprus and advising and representing various large publishing houses in Cyprus, Avgoustinos has gained thorough experience in defamation and libel cases. It should be mentioned that Avgoustinos has particular expertise and knowledge in handling defamation cases arising in the social media world, including Facebook (Meta). He has appeared numerous times before the Cyprus Tender Review Authority and has been advising clients on the procedural requirements of tendering in Cyprus. Furthermore, Avgoustinos has in-depth knowledge and experience in dealing with cases regarding construction law, including the creation and review of construction contracts and purchase agreements, acting from both the seller’s and the buyer’s side.

Moreover, Avgoustinos is a qualified arbitrator registered with the Cyprus Arbitration and Mediation Center (CAMC). This enables him to provide an alternative means of dispute resolution that encompasses multiple benefits to the parties. In particular, he has extensive knowledge in resolving employer-contractor disputes through arbitration.

  • Admitted to the Bar of Cyprus in 2004

The firm was founded in 1996 by Christos Patsalides under the name Christos Patsalides & Associates. Today it is located in the heart of Nicosia, very close to the courts and the business centre.

Its reputation for effective representation, through innovative and practical solutions, has made it a preferred partner for its clients. Their needs include dealing with increasingly complex regulation, addressing disputes, considering financial solutions in new markets and assessing new opportunities.

Today the firm’s client base includes both domestic and international clients, as well as the public sector.

Our philosophy

Our clients are the reason for our existence, and our attitude is shaped by the aim to be of value.

Our good reputation is of paramount importance to us. We strongly believe that honesty, integrity and transparency are the foundation that safeguards and enhances our good name.

The way we work is built on ensuring that everyone connected with the firm benefits from our presence.

Associated consulting company

Christos Patsalides Corporate Management Ltd

We expanded our firm through the establishment of Christos Patsalides Corporate Management Ltd, an associated consulting company. Through it, we offer accounting services and sophisticated corporate and financial advice.

The company has a deep understanding of the issues involved and long-lasting relationships with regulatory agencies, to ensure effective communication and efficient issue resolution.

Our commitments

The principles behind how we work

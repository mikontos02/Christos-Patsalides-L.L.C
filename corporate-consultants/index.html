Antis Kaplanis

CEO

As the CEO of Christos Patsalides Corporate Management Ltd, Antis brings the wealth of his managerial and financial expertise to the company, after serving for more than twenty years in senior positions in other fiduciary service providing firms, investment banking houses and commercial banks. He has advised large corporations in both Cyprus and Greece on a variety of matters including merger and acquisitions, capital restructurings, project financing through syndicate loans, and public listing and underwriting of their public offers addressing and complying with issues relating to regulatory, tax and anti- trust monopoly legislation. He has served on the Board of Directors of investment funds, regulated entities and public listed companies.

Savvas Orphanides

Financial and Regulatory Advisor

Savvas has extensive experience in Corporate Law with specific expertise and focus on the wider Banking, Financial and Insurance sectors. Throughout his career, Savvas has been an entrepreneur and an executive in the financial sector, and over the last ten years, he has been acting as an expert advisor on financial and regulatory matters to both local and international clients, including large private equity firms, financial services firms and corporates.

At the inception of his career, Savvas worked as an Investment Advisor at Morgan Stanley in New York and London in the Private Wealth Management. From 2000 up until 2013, he founded and managed two Financial Services Advisory firms, which he successfully sold. Since then, he has been an expert advisor to clients in the sector.

He is an active member of the private equity and business angel community in Cyprus and director and co-founder of the Cyprus Business Angels Network. Over the last twenty years, he has been a member of various listed companies and still is a member of a number of public and private companies particularly in the financial services sector and holds both the AMLCO and Advanced certificates issued by the Cyprus Securities and Exchange Commission.

Education
  • LLB (with Honours), University of Bristol, United Kingdom, 1993
  • Admitted to the Bar of Cyprus, Nicosia Chapter, in 1995
  • ACCI, UK Chartered Insurance Institute, 1995
  • MBA, Insead, France, 1997

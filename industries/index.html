Industries

Our experience in four industries: media and entertainment, energy, health care and pharmaceuticals, and real estate, each with a partner you can contact directly.

  • Areas of Practice
  • Corporate Management
  • Industries

01

Media & Entertainment

The media and entertainment industry is probably one of the fastest-evolving in the world, mainly because of advances in technology, and local and regional laws are evolving with it.

We have extensive knowledge of the industry and, over time, have advised the largest actors in the Cyprus market, offering unique solutions to new situations.

Libel, content regulation, IP rights and regulatory compliance are among the areas we routinely advise on.

Partner to contact

Avgoustinos Tsarkatzis

02

Energy

Our work in the energy sector includes transactional work involving photovoltaic parks, and helping clients navigate the extensive rules and regulations of the industry.

As the industry develops in the Cyprus market, both upstream and downstream, compliance is critical.

Our extensive network in the local market has proven effective in bringing stakeholders together to resolve issues and reach mutually acceptable agreements.

Partner to contact

03

Health Care and Pharmaceuticals

The pharmaceutical industry is one of the most regulated in the world.

Our firm has wide-ranging experience in the industry, including regulatory compliance, health insurance, acquisitions within the industry and patients’ rights.

We understand the business issues involved and strive to find the best solution to our clients’ needs.

Partner to contact

Constantinos Kokkinoftas

04

Real Estate

The real estate industry in Cyprus has grown rapidly over the last decade, and navigating the legal system is both complex and complicated.

We assist clients with any transaction they are contemplating, whether a sale, a purchase, a lease, financing or a foreclosure process, and advise on tax implications and environmental and land-use regulations.

We negotiate the terms and conditions of each transaction and safeguard our clients’ interests throughout its life cycle.

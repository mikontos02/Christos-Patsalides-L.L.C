Our story since 1996

Founded by Christos Patsalides, in the heart of Nicosia, close to the courts and the business centre.

The firm was established in 1996 as Christos Patsalides & Associates. Its reputation for effective representation through innovative and practical solutions has made it a trusted partner for clients facing complex regulation, disputes and new opportunities.

FAQ

Common questions, plain answers.

Where is your office?

Our office is at 31 Evagoras Avenue, Evagoras Building, 4th Floor, 1066 Nicosia, close to the courts and the business centre. Post can be sent to P.O. Box 25617, 1311 Nicosia.

Do you act for international clients?

Yes. Our client base includes both domestic and international clients, as well as the public sector.

Which areas of law do you practise?

Litigation, insurance, real estate, mass media and entertainment, family law, funds, mergers and acquisitions, corporate and commercial law, employment and labour law, immigration law, inheritance, estate planning and wills, and trusts. Each area is summarised above.

Can you also help with accounting and corporate administration?

Yes, through Christos Patsalides Corporate Management Ltd, our associated consulting company, which provides accounting services and corporate and financial advice.

Do you offer pro bono help?

We are committed to providing pro bono services to people in our community who would otherwise have no access to legal help. Please contact us to discuss your situation.

Does sending an enquiry make you my lawyer?

No. An enquiry through this website does not by itself create a lawyer–client relationship. Please avoid sharing confidential details until we have confirmed that we can act for you.

How can I reach you?

Call +357 22 677 677, email info@patsalides.com.cy, or use the contact form. Our fax number is +357 22 674 422.
